<div class="lang-en about-intro" markdown="1">

I am Meizhi Qian. My research lies at the intersection of applied mathematics, optimization, and control. I work primarily on PDE-constrained shape and topology optimization, with a particular focus on eigenvalue optimization, optimal design of networks, and the analysis and control of systems governed by beam, wave, Stokes, and p-Laplacian models.

From a methodological perspective, I am interested in topological derivatives, level set methods, and phase field approaches. A central theme in my work is to combine rigorous mathematical analysis with effective numerical algorithms, especially for problems where geometry, topology, and dynamics interact in a nontrivial way.

More broadly, I am interested in how structural design influences qualitative properties of PDE systems, including vibration behavior, stability, controllability, and long-time dynamics. My recent work reflects this direction through studies on eigenvalue optimization, Timoshenko beam networks, wave propagation on networks, and topology-based design methods for complex engineering structures.

</div>

<div class="lang-zh about-intro" markdown="1">

我是钱美智，研究方向主要位于应用数学、优化与控制的交叉领域。我关注偏微分方程约束下的形状优化与拓扑优化，特别是特征值优化、网络结构的最优设计，以及由梁、波动方程、Stokes 方程和 p-Laplacian 模型所描述系统的分析与控制。

在方法上，我主要使用拓扑导数、水平集方法和相场方法。我比较关注的一条主线，是如何将严格的数学分析与高效的数值算法结合起来，处理几何、拓扑与系统动力学紧密耦合的优化问题。

更具体地说，我希望理解结构设计如何影响偏微分方程系统的定性行为，例如振动特性、稳定性、可控性以及长时间行为。近年的工作主要围绕特征值优化、Timoshenko 梁网络、波动方程在网络上的控制，以及基于拓扑方法的复杂结构设计展开。

</div>
